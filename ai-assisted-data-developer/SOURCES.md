- The cost of goods sold accounts of the CRONUS localization in the sandbox.
- Whether the PBIP folder exported from Power BI Desktop imports as is with `fab import`, and whether a lakehouse with schemas lists its tables under `Tables/dbo` in `fab ls`.
- The compatibility level of a Direct Lake model created from Power BI Desktop, and the exact file form of `functions.tmdl` in a PBIP.
- Business Central mirroring to Fabric in the sandbox: not part of the demos.
